About Us

The team that brings process coordination and relocation under one roof. Who we are and how we work sits on this page.

Moving to Germany is more than filling in a form. We bring order to that journey.

Güven-Rhouta Solutions is a process coordination and relocation office: we run the cases of people coming to Germany to work, to study or to settle with their family, from start to finish. Visa and residence applications, recognition, translation and apostille, official appointments: whatever lies scattered, we gather into one plan and follow every step on the record.

The core of our work is order. Which paper goes when, to which office, in which sequence stands fixed from the start; correspondence, appointments and follow-ups turn on our desk. While you get ready for your new life, the case moves quietly forward.

Our Principles

01

The answer comes from the record

Which station your file is at, what comes next, which paper is awaited: when you ask, the record speaks, not a guess. Every step is written down, and we report at every one.

02

Scope and fee clear from the start

What we will do and what it costs, we settle together before the work begins. If the scope changes along the way, we agree it with you first; official fees never come to us and always go straight to the office.

03

The authority decides

On visas and residence the competent office has the last word; nobody can promise an outcome, and neither do we. Our word goes to the process: a complete file, fast answers, steady follow-up.

Three Languages, One Desk

We work in Turkish, German and English, and we keep the correspondence in whichever language suits you. Where language runs short, we come to the appointment with you: interpreting in the room and reading the papers to be signed together.

Whether you are still abroad or have lived in Germany for years, the order is the same and starts with a short message. From the first application through extensions to settlement, it continues at the same desk.

Our field is the process: set it up, run it, finish it.

Where your file calls for the legal assessment of an individual case, we put you in touch with lawyers practising in that field and keep coordinating the process.
